## Honest read

What works: real action titles with numbers, no eyebrows, title rule and footer rule with page numbers, direct-labelled charts without gridlines, one accent used on the bar that carries the message, bold figures in so-what boxes, footers and sources everywhere. This is a tidy, correct-looking house frame.

What stops a send: wrong units in the sensitivity table (IRR as dollars, multiples as percent, all-zero IRRs in deck 17), a title/body metric mismatch, English strings and misaligned tables inside the Arabic deck, an overlapping Arabic cover, and a strong rubber-stamp look: every deck is the same ten layouts in grey panels, and only the cover carries generated art. Many cards and panels are 60-70% empty.

## Cross-deck findings

- Four deck-level patterns repeat on every deck (cover + 2 numbered-list summary + KPI cards + chart-with-rail + table + cards + decision): reads as a template; visual range is limited to the cover art. Body slides use no generated art.
- The right-rail so-what box is the same grey rectangle at the same size on every chart slide and is often 60-70% empty; it makes slides look alike and wastes the story space.
- Chart-bearing slides show no gridlines and good direct labels (strength), consistent accent use on the bar that carries the message (strength), titles are mostly good action titles with numbers (strength).
- Rogo/Hebbia/FlashDocs comparison (judged from general knowledge of how they present, not from checked artefacts): their decks are typically firm-branded templates with editable native charts and a text-heavy bullet style; an Acretia deck beats a bullet-pitch on structure and numerical discipline, but any wrong unit (IRR table) or English leftovers in Arabic would lose to a plain but correct template. Parity item #41 (native, editable tables) looks met; no proof of brand templating (item #34) visible in these decks.

3. Cover frame/title fitter: measure Arabic and Latin line counts and reserve the subtitle y; fixes overlap on 14 cover, bad hyphen breaks ('sell-/side'), and 'one font stack' for Arabic headings.
4. Body-slide visual range: add a generated, deck-specific art/diagram layer (header band, in-slide illustration, icon system, football field, map) to at least 40% of content slides via the visuals styles; stop reusing the same grey-panel layout.

6. Panel sizing in the frame: so-what and card heights should be content-fitted (card_fit), not full height; stops 60-70% empty cards on steps, next-steps, decision, KPI and playbook archetypes.
7. Colour system for heatmaps/risk matrices/donuts: replace the cyan/periwinkle ramps with a single-hue ramp of the accent; one accent per slide, waterfall green/red/navy reduced to grey + accent + one negative.
8. Process/timeline archetype (chevron cards): too empty and unevenly centred; build a proper Gantt/timeline with week scale, gates and an icon per step; centre on the grid.
