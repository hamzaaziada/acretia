# S2 specialist review: harness, MCP servers, connectors, evidence chain, storage

Reviewer: independent senior platform and security engineer. Scope: `src/harness/{adapter,ledger,router,evidence}.py`, `src/harness/profiles/*`, `src/harness/mcp/*`, `src/harness/connectors/*`, `om/{llm,pii,replay,storage,contracts,deals}.py`. Date 2026-11-16. Offline only: fake transports, a local `http.server`, stdio subprocesses with `ACRETIA_MCP_ROOT` set to a temp dir. No keys used or added. No code, tests or gates changed. All scratch scripts live in the session scratchpad, not the repo.

## Verdict: FAIL (fix Critical and High, then re-review)   Score: 5/10

The bones are good: the adapter is sound on the happy path; path confinement, the zip traversal and bomb ratios, the CFB chain-cycle checks, the SSRF validator, key-host matching, notification handling, oversize framing and the 2-process evidence append all held. The failures sit in the places a bank buyer will probe first.

- The Grid MCP server's "read-only SELECT" guard is a keyword blacklist. It leaks any file the process can read, outside `ACRETIA_MCP_ROOT`, and one query hangs the server.
- The spend guard overshoots under concurrency and does not span processes.
- The evidence chain verifies after tail truncation and after a full re-chain, and `head_hash` is not anchored in DealMemory or delivery.
- `DealStore` silently loses concurrent updates.
- Several parsers amplify memory 100x to 500x from a small input.

Hebbia/Rogo parity needs tamper-evident evidence, bounded blast radius for untrusted input, and jobs that survive kills. Each of those has at least one reproduced gap below.

Counts: 1 Critical, 7 High, 15 Medium, 8 Low.

## Tested and held

Adapter and ledger:
- Retries are bounded at `max_retries+1` calls, and sleeps are capped at 30 s.
- A job is not lost on fallback for non-stream chat: a primary 503 fell through to the second adapter and the fallback events were reported.
- Schema repair: one retry; it works, and the ledger counts both calls.
- A streamed placeholder (`[EMAIL_1]`, `[PHONE_1]`) split every 1, 2 or 3 characters was reassembled and unmasked correctly. Unterminated `[` is released after 24 characters.
- Key host: lookalike (`llm.meshpool.ai.evil.com`), userinfo (`@evil.com`, `\@`), port 8443, trailing dot, plain http to localhost with a key were all refused. A mutated `base_url` is re-checked per call. CR/LF in a key is refused without echoing the value.
- A redirect to another hostname or another loopback IP is refused while a key is attached.
- Replay with `socket.socket.connect`, `create_connection` and `getaddrinfo` patched to raise: a hit was served with zero socket use; a miss raised `ReplayMiss` before any socket existed, and it does not fall through to fallbacks.
- Fixtures store hashes of prompts, not prompt text; the standard email, card, SSN, phone and IBAN did not reach the fixture or the transport.
- Spend guard: one-shot worst case over the limit was refused before sending (single-threaded); `BudgetExceeded` is not treated as fall-through.

MCP:
- Path confinement over stdio (excel server): `../x`, absolute path outside, symlink to outside, `ok.xlsx\x00.txt`, `root/../../x`, `/proc/self/root/...`, `ok.xlsx/../../x`, `~` were refused or not found. The `.` and `..` normalisation inside the root was accepted correctly.
- A notification (no `id`) carrying `tools/call` did not execute. Batch, non-object `params`, non-object `arguments`, bad JSON and invalid UTF-8 returned proper errors; an object `id` was rejected; 9 MB frames were drained and the stream stayed in sync (0.1 s); more than 5000 cells was refused.
- SQL guard: `attach`, `copy`, `install`, `glob`, `read_csv*`, `read_parquet`, `getenv` and `$$`-quoted keywords were blocked; `;`, comments and `from '...'` literals were blocked; DuckDB itself rejected writes through `query()`. (The bypasses are in F1.)

Connectors:
- Zip: `..`, absolute, `C:\`, backslash traversal, `a/./b/../../../x` rejected; percent-encoded and Unicode dots are inert names; case-duplicates written with a `__2` suffix; 1000:1 ratio rejected; a header lying about `file_size` (1000 vs 200 MB) was caught by CRC while streaming; 10,001 entries and nesting beyond 5 levels rejected.
- `.msg`: cyclic data chain and cyclic directory chain raise `UnpackError` in under 10 ms; a huge `n_dif` did not loop.
- Web: validator blocks `127.1`, `2130706433`, `0x7f.1`, `017700000001`, `0`, `[::ffff:7f00:1]`, `[::ffff:127.0.0.1]`, 6to4, Teredo, link-local metadata, CGNAT, benchmark nets, and fullwidth or circled-digit hosts (checked on the real resolved address). A resolver that flips to loopback on the second lookup is re-validated and blocked; connections are pinned to the validated IP; a redirect loop stops at 5 hops; `Content-Length` over the cap is refused.
- S3 SigV4: object keys are restricted to 64 lowercase hex, so odd-key encoding cannot arise; the prefix path is single-encoded identically in the canonical request and the URL. List queries sort identically raw and encoded for the fixed parameter names used.

Evidence and storage:
- 3 processes appending 300 records each to one bundle: 900 records, chain verified (flock works). A 1-byte edit, a deleted line, a swapped job file and a path-traversal payload hash are all detected.
- `AnalyticsDB` rejects identifiers with quotes, semicolons, spaces and leading digits; values always bind through `?`. (Only the trailing-newline quirk, F25.)
- `SQLiteKV`: 4 workers SIGKILLed 5 times mid-lease, 600 of 600 items processed, none missing, none duplicated.
