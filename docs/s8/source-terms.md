# S8.5 source terms register

Reviewed 2026-11-17 by fetching each terms or dataset page once with the identified client (`AcretiaResearch/1.0`). Labels: [OBSERVATION] fetched and read, [INFERENCE] our reading, [UNKNOWN] could not be confirmed. This is a log of what we found, not legal advice. Every connector honours robots.txt (a 401/403 or 5xx on robots.txt is a refusal), waits at least 2 s between requests to a host (the default `min_interval`) unless a source publishes a stricter limit, sends no email address in its User-Agent, and writes only under `data/raw/intl/<source>/` (git-ignored). German (Bundesanzeiger / Unternehmensregister) and Spanish (Registro Mercantil / BORME) accounts have no structured public data: connectors do not scrape them. Users upload the PDFs, or a connector hands a downloaded PDF to `om.intl.sources.routing.route_pdf_accounts`, which calls `om.intl.pipeline.read_report`.

Enabling the connectors: `import src.harness.connectors.intl_common` (extends the lazy registry so `base.get("egx")` etc. resolve), or call `src.harness.connectors.intl_common.load_all()`. Replay mode (`base.get("esef", replay=True)`) reads `tests/fixtures/intl_connectors/<source>/` and never touches the network.
