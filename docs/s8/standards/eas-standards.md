# Egyptian Accounting Standards and their IFRS counterparts

*Generated from `om/intl/standards/eas_standards.yaml`. Numbering marked unverified is from the 2015 reissue as recalled; confirm against FRA text.*

| 2 | Inventories | IAS 2 | Broadly the same: lower of cost and net realisable value, FIFO or weighted average, no LIFO. | unverified |

| 14 | Borrowing costs | IAS 23 | Aligned: capitalise on qualifying assets. | unverified |
| 15 | Related party disclosures | IAS 24 | Aligned. | unverified |
