# UK FRS 102 (The Financial Reporting Standard applicable in the UK and Republic of Ireland)

*Generated from `om/intl/standards/frameworks.yaml`. Authored by Acretia in its own words. Confidence: verified.*

**Issuer:** Financial Reporting Council (FRC)  
**Jurisdictions:** United Kingdom, Ireland

## Summary
A single reduced-disclosure standard for entities outside UK-adopted IFRS. The 2024 periodic review amendments, effective for periods starting on or after 1 January 2026, bring a five-step revenue model and on-balance-sheet lessee accounting close to IFRS 15 and IFRS 16. Older years show operating leases as rent expense.

## Relationship to full IFRS
Based on IFRS for SMEs with additions; permits policy choices (capitalise or expense development costs, amortise goodwill).

## Effective dates and caveats
- 2024 periodic review amendments: periods beginning on or after 1 Jan 2026, early adoption allowed

## Presentation
- **Profit or loss layout:** Formats prescribed by the Companies Act; by function (format 1) or by nature (format 2).
- **Balance sheet order:** Fixed assets, current assets and creditors split by one year, net current assets subtotal.
- **OCI:** Statement of comprehensive income or a single combined statement.

### Required line items
**IS**

- Revenue
- Finance costs
- Share of profit or loss of associates and joint ventures
- Tax expense
- Post-tax result of discontinued operations
- Profit or loss for the period
- Profit attributable to owners and to non-controlling interests

**BS**

- Property, plant and equipment
- Investment property
- Intangible assets
- Goodwill
- Investments accounted for using the equity method
- Other financial assets
- Inventories
- Trade and other receivables
- Cash and cash equivalents
- Assets held for sale
- Trade and other payables
- Provisions
- Financial liabilities (borrowings, lease liabilities)
- Current tax assets and liabilities
- Deferred tax assets and liabilities
- Non-controlling interests (within equity)
- Issued capital and reserves attributable to owners

**CF**

- Cash flows from operating activities
- Cash flows from investing activities
- Cash flows from financing activities
- Net change in cash and cash equivalents
- Effect of exchange rate changes on cash
- Cash and cash equivalents at start and end of period

### Cash flow choices
- Method: Indirect or direct; small entities exempt.
- Interest paid: Operating or financing.
- Interest received: Operating or investing.
- Dividends paid: Financing.
- Dividends received: Operating or investing.

## Measurement bases
- Historical cost with optional revaluation
- Goodwill and intangibles amortised over useful life (up to ten years when life cannot be reliably estimated)
- Choice of IFRS 9 or FRS 102 sections 11 and 12 for financial instruments

## Key disclosures an analyst reads
- Accounting policy choices (development cost, revaluation)
- Audit and directors remuneration disclosures required by the Companies Act
- Lease commitments under non-cancellable operating leases (before 2026)
